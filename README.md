# Shanklin Cafe Reimagined

Recreate the Shanklin Cafe website from https://shanklin-brunch-buddy.lovable.app/ with all pages, styling, and features:

Pages:
1. Home page (/):
   - Hero: "The first pour, in golden morning light." Subheading: "Slow-brewed single origin, a menu built on layers of flavour, and a room that feels like a long Sunday. Brunch in Hawthorn East, poured the way it should be." CTAs: "Book a table" and "See the menu".
   - Rating pill: 4.6 stars, 1,012 reviews.
   - Featured dishes: "What we're known for" featuring Chilli Prawn Scramble ($29), Baked Eggs Shakshuka ($27), and The Shanklin Flat White ($5.50).
   - Customer reviews section with verified local guide reviews.
   - "Hold your table" reservation CTA card.
   - Location, contact, and opening hours summary.

2. Menu page (/menu):
   - All Day Dining (served 7am until 2:30pm, seven days). Modern Australian brunch with Middle Eastern warmth.
   - Full breakfast, lunch, and specialty coffee sections with descriptions and pricing.

3. Story page (/story):
   - "Our story: Run by two friends who never left hospitality." Warm cafe philosophy, team story, and welcoming neighborhood cafe feel.

4. Visit page (/visit):
   - Location: 500 Tooronga Rd, Hawthorn East VIC 3123.
   - Phone: (03) 9882 7549 | Email: hello@shanklincafe.com.au.
   - Opening hours: Monday – Sunday 7:00am – 3:00pm.
   - Transit and parking info (corner of Tooronga Road, 2 minutes from tram stop).

5. Book a table page (/book):
   - 3-step table reservation flow: 1. Details (Date, time slot from 7:00am to 2:00pm, guest count), 2. Payment/Deposit ($10 per guest hold), 3. Confirmed screen.

Styling:
- Editorial cafe aesthetic with Fraunces serif headings and Work Sans body text, warm cream/espresso neutral palette, smooth interactive states, and responsive navigation header and footer.
